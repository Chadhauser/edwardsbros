The person behind the work

Peter Edwards ACMA CGMA

Chartered management accountant · career finance director · third-generation director of Edwards Bros (Spaldwick) Ltd

Peter Edwards is a chartered management accountant and a career finance director, with more than twenty-five years spent working in and on businesses — preparing their accounts, reading other people's, funding them, auditing them, and occasionally deciding they should never have been started. He is a third-generation director of Edwards Bros (Spaldwick) Ltd, the Huntingdonshire family company founded by four brothers in 1955, and the professional judgement behind its trading divisions.

That experience is the point of the company's businesses. Granton Finance packages lending cases the way credit committees actually read them. PassCyber approaches Cyber Essentials as a compliance discipline rather than an IT mystery. The data practice turns years of small-organisation records into reports a committee can act on. And the valuation and selling guides published here draw on the rarer perspective: Edwards Bros is itself a buyer of small businesses, so the advice is written by someone who sits on both sides of the table.
