Why a small supplier

What you get that you would not from a larger firm

On a contract of this size a large consultancy sends a partner to win it and a graduate to deliver it. Here the same person does both. Peter Edwards is a chartered management accountant who has been Head of Finance in businesses from £3 million to £15 million of turnover, and he is the person on the call, in the data and on the final report.

That means no account management layer, no handover between bid and delivery, and a direct line to someone who can make a decision. It also means we are honest about capacity. If a requirement is larger than we can deliver properly we will say so at the point of enquiry rather than at the point of failure.

We price fixed wherever the scope allows it, and we define completion in terms you can check rather than in days consumed.
