The company

A family company since 1955

Edwards Bros (Spaldwick) Ltd was founded in 1955 by four brothers as an agricultural and haulage business in Spaldwick, Huntingdonshire, and has remained in the same family ever since. For most of its history the company's business was the land: farming, then letting the farmland to a neighbouring farming business, alongside commercial buildings and, more recently, renewable generation on the estate.

The company today is more than its land. Its directors have built trading divisions around their own professional qualifications and experience — commercial finance broking, information security certification, publishing and data analysis — serving small businesses, charities and public bodies across Cambridgeshire, Northamptonshire and further afield.

The estate remains, still working, still held debt-free. But it is now one part of a company that trades.
