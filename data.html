The problem

Nobody looks at their data until it has to move

The method

Extract, profile, fix, prove, lock

The reason a trial balance has to agree to the penny is the same reason your product file has to agree with your sales ledger. This is reconciliation discipline applied to operational data, which is not how data cleaning is usually done — and it is why the report comes with numbers rather than reassurance.

Extract. You send an export, or we take a read-only copy. We never need access to your live system and we never write to it.

Profile. Every field is counted and tested before anything is changed, so we know the size of the problem before we touch it.

Fix. Duplicates merged, gaps filled, formats standardised, invalid codes corrected. Where two records might or might not be the same thing, we ask rather than decide.

Prove. The before-and-after counts and the full change log. You can see what happened to every record.

Lock. The validation rules that stop it happening again. We would rather not be needed twice for the same job.
